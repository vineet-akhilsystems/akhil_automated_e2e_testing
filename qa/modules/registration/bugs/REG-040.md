# Bug / Requirement Gap Report: REG-040 / REG-044 / REG-045 / REG-192 — Future DOB (`01/01/2030`), Extreme Ages (`1900` / `150Y`), and 30-Year-Old "New Born" Registrations Are Accepted and Saved

- **Case IDs:** `REG-040`, `REG-044`, `REG-045`, `REG-192`, `REG-141`
- **Module:** Patient Registration — DOB & Age Validation (`DateField.jsx`, `RegistrationPreview.jsx`, Webpart Config)
- **Severity:** High (Clinical & Demographic Data Integrity Defect)
- **Priority:** P1
- **Environment:** `https://demo.augastam.com/registration` (Build `2.0.66`, Tenant: `Miracle HIS Demo`)
- **Status:** Confirmed Validation & Configuration Defect (Verified Live with Created UHIDs)

---

## 1. Summary
The Patient Registration module accepts and saves clinically impossible Dates of Birth and Ages across both **New Registration** and **New Born Registration**:
1. **`REG-040` (Future DOB Accepted):** Entering `01/01/2030` in **DOB** and clicking **Save** succeeds and creates patient **UHID `ASNR0000000428`**.
2. **`REG-044` (Year 1900 / Age 126Y Accepted):** Entering `01/01/1900` in **DOB** and clicking **Save** succeeds and creates patient **UHID `ASNR0000000429`**.
3. **`REG-045` (Age 150Y Accepted):** Typing `150` into **Age** (`150Y 0M 0D` -> auto-calculates DOB `29/10/1876`) and clicking **Save** succeeds and creates patient **UHID `ASNR0000000430`**.
4. **`REG-192` (30-Year-Old Registered as "New Born"):** Selecting **New Born Registration**, entering DOB `15/08/1996` (30 years old) with Mother UHID `ASNR0000000445`, and clicking **Save** succeeds and creates newborn record **UHID `ASNBR240000000009`**.
5. **`REG-141` (Non-Leap Day `29/02/2001` Silently Mutated):** Typing `29/02/2001` (2001 is not a leap year) silently rewrites the value to `28/02/2001` without notifying the user.

## 3. Root Cause & Code Logic
1. **Webpart Config Has No Date Constraint (`webpart_latest_version__5b5bc4c1-bafb-4432-b3f2-caaa0a4c2e7f.json`):**
   - In the live `Basic Information` webpart config, the `DOB` field (`id: 5`, `column: "dob"`) has `"dateOption": ""` (No Constraint) and `"futureDates": false` is not enforced when `dateOption` is empty.
2. **`DateField.jsx` Does Not Reject Typed Future Dates (`custom-form-builder/src/components/registration/builder/DateField.jsx:114-182`):**
   - Even when `maxDate` is passed to `ReactDatePicker`, the custom text input handler (`CustomInput.handleInputChange`) parses `DD/MM/YYYY` and emits `yyyy-MM-dd` via `onChange` without checking whether the typed date exceeds `today` (`new Date()`) or falls before a minimum human birth year (e.g., 120 years ago).
3. **Save Handler (`RegistrationPreview.jsx:2882-2925`) & Backend (`patient-registration.controller.ts:2310+`) Do Not Validate Date Bounds:**
   - Neither `validateRequiredFields()` on the frontend nor `createPatientRegistration` on the backend checks `dob <= today`, maximum age (`<= 120Y`), or Newborn age limits (`<= 28 days` / `<= 1 year`) when `registrationType === 'New Born Registration'`.

---

## 4. Recommended Fix
1. **Webpart Configuration:** Set `DOB` Date Selection option to **Past + Today** (`pastDates` / `maxDate = today`).
2. **Frontend (`DateField.jsx` & `RegistrationPreview.jsx`):**
   - In `DateField.jsx`, reject or clamp typed dates where `parsedDate > new Date()` when future dates are disabled, and validate `age <= 120` years.
   - In `RegistrationPreview.jsx`, block Save if `dob > today`, `age > 120`, or if `New Born Registration` has a DOB older than the hospital's newborn threshold.
3. **Backend (`patient-registration.controller.ts`):** Reject payloads where `dob > today` with `BadRequestException`.
