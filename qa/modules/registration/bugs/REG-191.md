# Bug / Requirement Gap Report: REG-191 — New Born Registration Accepts Non-Existent Mother UHID (`ASNR9999999999`)

- **Case ID:** `REG-191`
- **Module:** Patient Registration — New Born Registration (`Mother's UHID` field & `POST /patient-registration/create`)
- **Severity:** High (Orphaned Newborn Record Linked to Invalid Mother UHID)
- **Priority:** P1
- **Environment:** `https://demo.augastam.com/registration` (Build `2.0.66`, Tenant: `Miracle HIS Demo`)
- **Status:** Confirmed Backend & Frontend Validation Defect (Verified Live with Created UHID `ASNBR240000000008`)

---

## 1. Summary
In **New Born Registration**, the **Mother's UHID*** field is marked mandatory (`REG-080`), and entering a valid existing patient UHID works (`REG-190` -> `ASNBR240000000007`). However, entering a completely fabricated, non-existent UHID (`ASNR9999999999`) into **Mother's UHID*** is accepted without any lookup or existence verification, creating an orphaned newborn record (**UHID `ASNBR240000000008`**).

---

## 2. Steps to Reproduce
1. Open Patient Registration (`/registration/1101f999-7c91-4ba5-a52b-a80d728c588e`).
2. Switch **Registration Type** to `New Born Registration`.
3. Fill mandatory fields (`Title: Baby of`, `First Name`, `Last Name`, `Gender`, `DOB`, `Mobile No.`, `Address`).
4. In **Mother's UHID***, type `ASNR9999999999` (which does not exist in `PATIENT_REGISTRATION_DB`).
5. Click **Save**.

---

## 3. Expected vs. Actual Result
- **Expected:**
  - The frontend/backend verifies that `Mother's UHID` exists in `PATIENT_REGISTRATION_DB` (and belongs to a Female patient of plausible maternal age) before creating the newborn registration.
- **Actual:**
  - The form accepts `ASNR9999999999` as plain text and saves the newborn registration (**UHID `ASNBR240000000008`**) with `HTTP 201`.

---

## 4. Recommended Fix
In `akhil_systems_backend/src/patient-registration/patient-registration.controller.ts` (and/or via a UHID lookup field in the `Basic Information` webpart), verify that `mother_uhid` references an existing active patient record in `PATIENT_REGISTRATION_DB` before persisting a `New Born Registration`.
